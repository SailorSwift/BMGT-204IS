## The weekly rhythm — never violate it

- **Tuesday** introduces a method: where it comes from, what it assumes, what it is for.
- **Thursday** opens with a **10-minute quiz** on the previous week, then practices — usually a lab.
- Every deliverable is due **Tuesday at the start of class**, on Canvas.
- Exceptions with no quiz: Exam 1 (Oct 1), Exam 2 (Nov 12), last day (Dec 10).

So a Thursday plan has only **65 usable minutes** after the quiz. A Tuesday plan has 75.

## Calendar realities to plan around

- **Sep 1** last day to self-add; **Sep 9** last day to add via instructor and to self-drop; **Sep 16** last day to drop without a "W". Sessions 2–3 contain students who missed session 1. Form no durable groups before Sep 15.
- **Nov 18** last day to drop with a "W" — six days after Exam 2, deliberately.
- **No meetings lost to holidays.** Labor Day and Veterans Day both fall on Wednesdays in 2026. Only Fall Break (Nov 23–27) removes classes, and it removes no Tue/Thu meeting from the numbered list above.
- Over-enrolled and half the meetings are laptop labs: **verify seating and power outlets** in JABS 215.

## Materials actually in this repo — verify before you cite

**Cite no path you have not confirmed exists.** A lesson plan that sends the
instructor to a file that is not there fails in front of the room.

**Consequence for planning sessions 8 and later:** there is no publisher deck
and no dataset folder in the repo for Ch 5–17 yet. Do not fabricate paths.
Either plan around the textbook PDF in `Books/` and datasets you construct, or
say plainly in the plan that the Ch *N* materials need to be added first and
list exactly what is needed.

**Office temp files.** `~$*.pptx` / `~$*.xlsx` are Office lock artifacts from an
open file, not content. Never cite one, and flag them for deletion before
anything is posted to Canvas.

## Established course conventions

Decisions already made in Unit 1. Later plans must not contradict them.

- `QUARTILE.INC` is the course convention, because it matches ToolPak output.
- `.S` sample variants (`VAR.S`, `STDEV.S`) are the default; the *n*−1 reason is deferred to Unit 3 and that deferral is stated out loud.
- Population vs sample notation is **μ, σ, π** against **x̄, s, p**, introduced session 2 and never allowed to slip.
- Reproducibility means **live formulas, not pasted values** — worth 10 points on every project.
- The semester's through-line: *what can I responsibly conclude from imperfect data?*
