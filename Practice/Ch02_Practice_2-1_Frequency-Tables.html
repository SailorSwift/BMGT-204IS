Soft Drink Purchases

Data have been collected from a sample of 50 soft drink purchases. Each observation indicates the brand purchased: Coca-Cola, Diet Coke, Dr. Pepper, Pepsi, or Sprite.

Check yourself

Your frequency column must total 50, your relative frequencies must total 1.00, and your percent frequencies must total 100. If a total is off, the error is in the counting, not the arithmetic.

In Excel, build the frequency column with a COUNTIF against the brand column or with a PivotTable, then divide each count by the total using an absolute reference so the formula copies down cleanly.
